# Luciole Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 3 October 2026.*

Luciole is an Android app of bedtime stories, read aloud by a parent to their child. It is published by its author, as an individual.

## What the app collects

Luciole itself collects, sends and shares no personal data.

- There is **no account**, no sign-up, no login, **no advertising**, no audience analytics, no crash reports sent.
- Paid worlds are bought through **Google Play**. Payment happens at Google: Luciole never sees a card, a name or an address. The purchase is handled under [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- For these purchases, Luciole includes the **Google Play Billing Library**. It sends Google technical information about its own operation (whether a call to the Play Store succeeded, whether the connection failed), which Google uses to improve billing. It brings two Android permissions: Google Play billing, and Internet access for this technical information. Luciole’s own code opens no connection at all: a check on every build of the app enforces it.

## What the app keeps on the phone

To work, Luciole stores on the device, and only there:

- the child’s name and the chosen pronoun (“she” or “he”), to place them in the story;
- the choices made before each reading (hero, companion, place) and the preferred text size;
- the history of stories read, with the ending reached, and the page where a reading was interrupted.

This information never leaves the phone. It is erased with the app when it is uninstalled, and the name can be changed at any time from the home screen.

## Children

Luciole is meant for parents, who read, and for children aged five to eight, who listen and choose. Luciole collects no data about children: the name, the choices and the history stay on the phone.
