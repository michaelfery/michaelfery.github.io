# Privacy Policy — Bookfolio

**Last updated: April 6, 2026**

Lire cette page en [français](/bookfolio/privacy-fr/).

---

## 1. Introduction

Bookfolio (the "Application") is developed and published as an individual project. This privacy policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and what rights you have as a user.

By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this document.

---

## 2. Data collected

### 2.1 Google account data

When you sign in via **Google Sign-In**, the Application accesses basic information from your Google account: name, email address, and profile picture. This data is used solely to identify your session and synchronise your library.

No Google password is ever stored by the Application.

### 2.2 Book library

Books you add to your library (title, author, ISBN, notes, reading status, etc.) are stored **locally on your device** and, if you enable synchronisation, **in your personal Google Drive account**.

This data is never transmitted to third-party servers other than Google Drive.

### 2.3 Camera

The Application requests camera access **solely to scan ISBN barcodes** (EAN-13). The camera is activated only when the user explicitly opens the scanner. No images or video are recorded or transmitted.

Barcode recognition is performed **locally on the device** using **Google ML Kit**, without sending any data to external servers.

### 2.4 Notifications

The Application may request permission to send **local notifications** (reminders, confirmations). No notifications are sent from a remote server.

### 2.5 Network

The Application uses the Internet to:
- Search for books via the **Google Books API** (queries by title, author or ISBN)
- Synchronise your library with **Google Drive**
- Load book cover images

Requests to the Google Books API transmit only the search terms you enter.

### 2.6 Optional telemetry (opt-in only)

Bookfolio includes Firebase telemetry for product quality:
- **Firebase Analytics** (anonymous feature usage and app version metrics)
- **Firebase Crashlytics** (crash diagnostics)

Telemetry is **disabled by default**. It is enabled only if you explicitly opt in from onboarding or settings. You can disable it at any time.

---

## 3. Data not collected

Without telemetry opt-in, the Application does **not** collect any of the following:

- Location data
- Contacts
- Advertising identifiers
- Biometric data
- Browsing history
- Performance or crash data

---

---

## 5. Storage and security

- Local data is stored in the Application's private database (Room/SQLite), inaccessible to other applications.
- Synchronised data is stored in your own Google Drive and governed by Google's security policies.
- The Application has no proprietary server.

---

## 6. Data retention

Data is retained for as long as the Application is installed on your device. Uninstalling the Application deletes all local data. Data stored in Google Drive remains under your control and can be deleted from your Google account at any time.

---

## 7. User rights

In accordance with the GDPR (where applicable), you have the following rights:

- **Access**: view the data held about you
- **Rectification**: correct your data directly within the Application
- **Erasure**: delete your library from the Application or from Google Drive
- **Portability**: your data is directly accessible via Google Drive

---

## 8. Children

The Application is not intended for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
